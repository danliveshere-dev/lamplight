# Origin

Lamplight began as the visual and interaction language inside Daius, a personal
AI companion project. This repository extracts the reusable design-system
work without exporting Daius itself, its personal data, its automation or its
private operating environment.

The names used for Lamplight's materials, including tallow, phosphor, ember and
hearth, are part of the design language. Consumer names and consumer-specific
tokens are not part of the public core.
